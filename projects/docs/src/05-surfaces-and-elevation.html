Surfaces and elevation

Backgrounds for the page and the things on it, the text colours to use on them, and six shadow depths.

The surface ladder

Surface, outline and background tokens are roles, not palettes: they sit outside chapter 4's grammar and take no variant and no --contrast. Their text colours are the on- tokens in the next section.

Surfaces are near-grey shades of the neutral palette from chapter 3, so they carry a trace of your primary's hue. --color--surface is the page. --color--surface-dim and --color--surface-bright are a darker and a lighter page background: in light mode bright equals surface and dim is darker; in dark mode dim equals surface and bright is lighter. Use them for a backdrop that should recede or stand out.

Five container shades stack on top. Lowest to highest is emphasis, not lightness: lowest is white in light mode and near-black in dark, and each step up is a little darker in light mode and a little lighter in dark, like higher in chapter 4.

Token Use
--color--surface-container-lowest Cards and sheets.
--color--surface-container-low Grouped content behind cards.
--color--surface-container Toolbars, navigation.
--color--surface-container-high Chips, inline code, selected rows.
--color--surface-container-highest Input fields, the strongest fill.

The order holds in both modes, so a card stays distinct from the page when the mode flips.

Text on surfaces

--color--on-surface reads at 4.5:1 or better on every surface above, in both modes, whatever primary you set. --color--on-surface-variant is a muted text colour for captions and secondary lines. Surfaces are not coloured boxes in chapter 3's sense: on-surface is already full strength, so small text needs nothing more.

--color--background and --color--on-background are other names for surface and on-surface, for stylesheets that prefer those names.

Surface variant

--color--surface-variant is a more tinted fill from the neutral-variant palette. Its text colour is --color--on-surface-variant.

Outlines

--color--outline is for borders that carry meaning, such as the edge of a text field. --color--outline-variant is for quieter, decorative dividers.

Elevation

--elevation-0 to --elevation-5 are box-shadow values of increasing depth; they are not colours, so their names have no --color-- part. Zero is none. The example labels a typical use for each: 0 flat, 1 card, 2 raised, 3 menu, 4 sheet, 5 dialog.

The shadow colour is --elevation-shadow-color, the same black in both modes; set it on :root to tint every shadow. Elevation is a shadow only: it does not change the surface colour. Every card in the example uses --color--surface-container-lowest, so only the shadow differs; combine elevation with a container shade to set a card further apart from the page.
