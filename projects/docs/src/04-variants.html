Variants

Each palette colour comes with a family of variants, every one with its own contrast colour. The names follow one grammar.

Switch between Light and Dark at the top of the page to see which rows move and which stay.

Lighter and darker

-lighter-1, -lighter-2 mix the colour toward white; -darker-1, -darker-2 toward black. They mean the same in both modes: darker is always darker.

Higher and lower

-higher-1, -higher-2 stand out more against the page; -lower-1, -lower-2 less. In light mode higher is darker; in dark mode higher is lighter. Use these for hover and pressed states, so a control that darkens on hover in light mode brightens in dark mode without a second rule.

Reverse mode

-reverse-mode gives the other mode's value: the dark primary while the page is light, and the light primary while it is dark. It is how you build a deliberately inverted element, such as a dark toast on a light page, that still inverts correctly when the page flips.

Tints

-tint-1 to -tint-9 are the colour at nine opacities, from 2% to 90%, painted over whatever is behind. Tints 2, 3 and 4 match the common hover, focus and drag overlay strengths. Tints have no contrast colour; put text on the element behind them.

Disabled

-disabled desaturates the colour and fades it, and -disabled--contrast fades the text with it. It combines with any variant: --color--primary-darker-1-disabled.

Contrast with emphasis

Every colour and variant has three contrast colours. --contrast--high is pure black or white and reads at 4.5:1 or better on its colour, which is the WCAG AA level for normal text. --contrast is blended 10% toward the colour and reads at 3:1 or better, the AA level for large text and interface parts. --contrast--low is blended further and is decorative: use it for hints and dividers, not for text that must be read. The percentages are tunable, see Tuning.

The name grammar

--color--{palette}[-{variant}][-reverse-mode][-disabled][--contrast[--high|--low]]

Single dashes join the parts of one colour; a double dash introduces its contrast. {variant} is one of lighter-1, lighter-2, darker-1, darker-2, higher-1, higher-2, lower-1, lower-2, or tint-1 to tint-9. Tints take no -disabled and no --contrast. The Reference draws all of them.
